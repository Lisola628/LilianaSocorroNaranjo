Spanish educator, curriculum developer, and scholar

Baton Rouge, Louisiana

Spanish educator, curriculum developer, and scholar with more than a decade of university teaching at Tulane University and Louisiana State University. Research examines Andean Indigenous religious traditions, colonial cultural translation, and contemporary Indigenous cultural practices; current work includes a book under contract with the University of Alabama Press. Former Assistant Coordinator of Tulane’s Spanish Basic Language Program, with experience in curriculum revision, assessment, faculty mentoring, and open educational resources. Teaching emphasizes communication through cultural inquiry, community engagement, and projects that allow students to demonstrate their learning in different ways.
